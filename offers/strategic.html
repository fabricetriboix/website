Strategic

Description

Strategic carries out the tasks in the statement of work and leaves the evidence with the people who have to use it. I implement those tasks to the acceptance criteria. Where it is feasible and applicable I add automated unit tests, functional tests, non-functional tests, stress tests and integration tests.

Where it applies I add a canary and I connect the feature to your observability stack, so an alert is raised as soon as the feature has a problem in production. The documentation includes how to make changes in the future. I write runbooks for the incident team, and we run a live failure drill with that team.

You receive a leadership briefing pack explaining how risks have been mitigated, with evidence and next priorities, ready for the board. You also receive an audit evidence pack mapped to operational resilience rules, ready for auditors and regulators.

Handover calls continue for as long as they are needed, and support lasts three months with a guaranteed response time. All the deliverables completed, or I work for free until they are.
