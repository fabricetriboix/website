Platform Compass

Indicative duration

2 days to 2 weeks, depending on the size of the platform.

Description

Platform Compass is an initial assessment of the platform or cloud infrastructure, through inspection of live resources, infrastructure as code analysis and interviews. It is fact-finding only. I make no changes to the infrastructure.

What you will have

  • A clear understanding of the risks related to your cloud infrastructure, prioritised by likelihood and business impact.
  • A roadmap with effort estimates showing how to address those risks, how to optimise cloud costs and how to remove pain points, apply industry practice and improve the infrastructure.

Scope

  • Identify risks.
    • Ability to recover from disasters.
    • Technical debt.
    • Availability.
    • Change management, including deployments and rollbacks.
    • Tenant isolation.
    • Cybersecurity.
    • Gaps in observability and alerting.
    • Compliance and regulatory risks.
    • Knowledge transfer and organisational risks.
    • Whether the infrastructure can scale as the business grows.
  • Suggest mitigations.
  • Identify pain points and inefficiencies, and suggest mitigations.
  • Research cloud cost optimisations.
  • Review the infrastructure as code.
  • Opportunities to apply industry practice.

Deliverables

  • A written report with prioritised risks, prioritised tasks and suggested mitigations with effort estimates.
  • Diagrams and other artefacts as necessary.
  • A wrap-up call.
  • Suggested next steps.
